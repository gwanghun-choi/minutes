## Decisions

No decision records were written. The MVP choices — raw SQL over an ORM,
`BackgroundTasks` over a queue, no RAG framework, BGE-M3 retained, CPU/int8
inference — were made under an explicit "no over-engineering, finish today"
constraint and are captured as invariants in `AGENTS.md`. Back-filling
retrospective records would manufacture authority the reasoning did not have.

Policy for future decisions: [../decisions/README.md](../decisions/README.md).
